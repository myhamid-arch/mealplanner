- 100 g Tomato
- 4 g Cumin
- 4 g Coriander seed
- 2 g Cardamom
- 2 g Cinnamon
- 3 g Turmeric
- 2 g Black pepper
- 1 g Cloves
- 100 g Onion
- 30 g Raisins
- 10 g Olive oil
- 1000 g Chicken stock (cooking liquid, absorbed)
- 5 g Salt
Method:
1. Rinse and soak the rice for 20 minutes, then drain.
2. Bring the stock (topped up with the chicken's pan juices) to a boil with the remaining spices, the chopped tomato and salt.
3. Add the rice, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 14 minutes. Rest for 5 minutes and fluff.
4. Meanwhile cook the sliced onion in the olive oil over medium-low heat for 15 minutes until soft and golden, add the raisins for 2 minutes, and fold through the rice.

Warm fava beans mashed with cumin, garlic and lemon, olive oil to finish, with boiled eggs, bread and vegetables.
Cuisine: levantine / emirati_gulf. Slots: breakfast. Packable: no. Served cold: no.

### Foul medames (carb; 80–350 g cooked per plate, default 180 g)

**Warm, lightly mashed** (stewed, default; batch ≈ 1252 g cooked; 15 min)
Ingredients:
- 1000 g Foul medames, canned
- 15 g Garlic
- 4 g Cumin
- 50 g Lemon juice
- 150 g Tomato
- 20 g Parsley, fresh
- 5 g Salt
Method:
1. Warm the fava beans with their liquid for 5 minutes, then drain, keeping 100 ml of the liquid.
2. Mash roughly with the crushed garlic, cumin, lemon juice and salt, loosening with the liquid.
3. Top with the diced tomato and chopped parsley.

**Smooth** (blended; batch ≈ 1244 g cooked; 12 min)
Ingredients:
- 1000 g Foul medames, canned
- 15 g Garlic
- 4 g Cumin
- 50 g Lemon juice
- 150 g Tomato
- 20 g Parsley, fresh
- 5 g Salt
Method:
1. Warm the fava beans with their liquid for 5 minutes and drain.
2. Blend with the garlic, cumin, lemon juice, salt and a little of the liquid until smooth.
3. Top with the diced tomato and chopped parsley.

## Lean beef burger with sweet potato wedges (beef-burger)
Grilled lean beef burger in a toasted bun with lettuce, tomato and burger sauce, and sweet potato wedges.
Cuisine: american. Slots: lunch, dinner. Packable: no. Served cold: no.

### Beef patty (protein; 70–250 g cooked per plate, default 130 g)

**Grilled** (grilled, default; batch ≈ 879 g cooked; 15 min)
Ingredients:
- 1000 g Beef mince, 95% lean
- 80 g Onion
- 10 g Worcestershire sauce
- 8 g Salt
- 2 g Black pepper
Method:
1. Mix the mince with the grated onion, Worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper. Shape into 130 g patties 1.5 cm thick with a dimple in the centre.
2. Grill over high heat for 4 minutes per side until 71 °C inside.

**Smash-seared** (pan_seared; batch ≈ 790 g cooked; 12 min)
Ingredients:
- 1000 g Beef mince, 95% lean
- 80 g Onion
- 10 g Worcestershire sauce
- 8 g Salt
- 2 g Black pepper
Method:
1. Mix the mince with the grated onion, Worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper. Roll into 65 g balls.
2. Press each ball flat onto a smoking-hot cast-iron pan and sear for 2 minutes per side until crusted and 71 °C inside.

### Toasted bun (carb; 40–100 g cooked per plate, default 70 g)

**Toasted** (grilled, default; batch ≈ 410 g cooked; 2 min)
Ingredients:
- 500 g Burger bun
Method:
1. Split the buns and toast cut side down on the grill for 1 minute.

### Sweet potatoes (carb; 80–300 g cooked per plate, default 150 g, optional)

**Roasted** (roasted, default; batch ≈ 770 g cooked; 40 min)
Ingredients:
- 1000 g Sweet potato
- 20 g Olive oil
- 3 g Cumin
- 6 g Salt
Method:
1. Heat the oven to 220 °C (fan 200 °C).
2. Cut the sweet potatoes (skin on) into 3 cm chunks and toss with the olive oil, cumin and salt.
3. Roast on a lined tray for 30–35 minutes, turning once, until caramelised at the edges.

**Air-fried wedges** (air_fried; batch ≈ 762 g cooked; 25 min)
Ingredients:
- 1000 g Sweet potato
- 12 g Sunflower oil
- 3 g Cumin
- 6 g Salt
Method:
1. Cut the sweet potatoes into wedges 2 cm thick and toss with the sunflower oil, cumin and salt.
2. Air-fry at 200 °C for 18–20 minutes, shaking every 6 minutes.

### Lettuce, tomato and onion (vegetable; 30–120 g cooked per plate, default 60 g, optional)

**Fresh** (raw, default; batch ≈ 700 g cooked; 5 min)

### Burger sauce (sauce; 10–50 g cooked per plate, default 25 g, optional)

**Burger sauce** (raw, default; batch ≈ 335 g cooked; 3 min)
Ingredients:
- 150 g Greek yogurt, plain, fat free
- 80 g Mayonnaise

Method:

Assembly:
